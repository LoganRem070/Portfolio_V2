Projects
Rosary App
Built during Hack.Coms 2026, this app came from a direct need that the Catholic Newman community at RIT had. When the last member who our favorite rosary app on their phone graduated, since it was taken off the app store we were stuck. So me and Ryan Ackerley got to work and made a new version for us to use. It was built using React Native, Java Spring Boot, and TigerData. It has built in translation and its being used weekly by the Newman community to pray the rosary together.
